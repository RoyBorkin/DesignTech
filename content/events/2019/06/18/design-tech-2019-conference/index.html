Design Tech 2019: International Conference on Design and Technology

2019-06-18 – 2019-06-19 · Design Tech 2019: הכנס הבינלאומי לעיצוב וטכנולוגיה

The Design-Tech 2019 conference was a new platform for researchers and practitioners in design, held for the first time at the Technion. The proceedings were organised in five themes: Design as a Hacker, Design as a Protector, Design as a Healer, Design as a Visualizer and Design as a Leader.

Conference chair: Prof. Ezri Tarazi.
