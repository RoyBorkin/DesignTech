Oren Arbel

PhD researcher · Ceramic designer

Supervisors: Prof. Ezri Tarazi

A ceramic designer researching participatory design. Oren led the lab's reef restoration project with the local community on Beqa Island, Fiji, where reef units were produced by clay extrusion and local firing methods.

Keywords: Participatory design, Ceramics, Community
